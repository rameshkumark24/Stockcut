The short version

StockCut works entirely on your device. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server belonging to us. Your jobs — the stock lengths, the pieces, the labels you type — are stored only on your phone and are never sent anywhere.

Three third-party services do collect some data, and they are listed below.

What stays on your device and never leaves it

  • Every saved job: names, stock lengths, part lengths, quantities and labels
  • Your settings: preferred units, fraction denominator, saw kerf, theme

We cannot see any of this. It is held in the app's own private storage. If you uninstall the app, it is removed, subject to any Android backup you have enabled in your own Google account settings.

What is collected, and by whom

ServiceWhat it collectsWhy
Google AdMob Advertising ID and related device information, in line with the consent choice you make when you first open the app To show ads in the free version. Ads are not shown if you buy the one-time unlock.
Firebase Crashlytics Crash reports: the error, the app version, the Android version, the device model, and the state of the app at the moment it crashed To find and fix crashes. It contains no personal details and no contents of your jobs.
Google Play Billing Handles the purchase itself. We never see or store your payment details. To sell the one-time unlock.

The feedback form

If you choose to report a problem or suggest a feature, the app opens a Google Form in your browser. It is optional and entirely user-initiated — nothing is sent unless you fill it in and submit it.

The form asks what happened, and what you were cutting. It also pre-fills a single diagnostics line that looks like this:

v1.0.3 (12) | Android 13 | SM-A515F | mm | free | 47 optimizes

That line is shown on screen and you can edit or delete it before sending. It contains the app version, your Android version, your device model, which measurement units you are using, whether you are on the free or paid version, and how many times you have used the optimizer.

The form has no name field, no email field, and no contact field of any kind. That is deliberate. It also means we cannot reply to a form submission — if you want an answer, use the "Email support" link instead, which opens your own mail app so you decide what to share.

The diagnostics line never contains your advertising ID, any install identifier, your location, or anything from your saved jobs.

Google processes form responses on our behalf. We keep them for 24 months and then delete them.

Children

StockCut is a tool for tradespeople and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

  • Ads: you can change your ad consent choice at any time from Settings inside the app.
  • Advertising ID: Android lets you reset or delete it in your device settings.
  • Your jobs: delete any job in the app, or uninstall the app to remove all of them.
  • Feedback: simply do not submit the form.

Changes

If what the app collects ever changes, this page and the Play Store data safety declaration are updated together, before the change ships.
