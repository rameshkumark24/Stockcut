Privacy Policy — StockCut

Last updated: 09-08-2026 · Contact: rameshkumaroff@gmail.com

The short version

StockCut works entirely on your device. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server belonging to us. Your jobs — the stock lengths, the pieces, the labels you type — are stored on your phone and are never sent to us. One copy can leave your phone: Android's own backup, into your own Google Drive. That is described in full below.

We collect nothing from you — there is no form, no survey and no submission of any kind. Two third-party services used by the app do collect some data, and they are listed below.

What stays on your device

  • Every saved job: names, stock lengths, part lengths, quantities and labels
  • Your settings: preferred units, fraction denominator, saw kerf, theme

We cannot see any of this and it is never uploaded to us. There is no account, no server of ours, and nothing to sign in to. It is held in the app's own private storage on your phone.

Android backup — the one copy that leaves your phone

If Android's backup is switched on in your Google account settings — it is on by default for most phones — Android copies your saved jobs and settings into your own Google Drive backup, exactly as it does for other apps on your phone. This is why your jobs come back when you reinstall StockCut or move to a new phone.

That copy is yours, not ours. We cannot read it, list it, or recover it for you. It sits in your Google account, it does not count against your Drive storage, and on current Android versions it is encrypted with your device PIN or passcode.

You can switch it off at any time in Android's Settings → Google → Backup. If you do, your jobs live only on this phone — and losing or replacing the phone will lose them, because we have no copy to restore from.

What is collected, and by whom

ServiceWhat it collectsWhy
Google AdMob Advertising ID and related device information, in line with the consent choice you make when you first open the app To show ads. StockCut is free, and ads are the only thing funding it — there is no paid version and nothing in the app is for sale.
Firebase Crashlytics Crash reports: the error, the app version, the Android version, the device model, and the state of the app at the moment it crashed To find and fix crashes. It contains no personal details and no contents of your jobs.

Feedback and support

StockCut has no feedback form and collects nothing you submit. There is no bug-report form, no suggestion box, and no in-app survey.

The only way to contact us is the "Email support" button, which opens your own mail app with an empty message. You write it, you can see and delete everything in it, and nothing reaches us unless you press send. That message is between you and us by ordinary email — we do not collect it through the app.

For convenience the app offers to add one line to that email:

v1.0.3 (12) | Android 13 | SM-A515F | mm | free | 47 optimizes

It is shown on screen and you can delete it before sending. It contains the app version, your Android version, your device model, which measurement units you are using, and how many times you have used the optimizer. It never contains your advertising ID, any install identifier, your location, or anything from your saved jobs.

Children

StockCut is a tool for tradespeople and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

  • Ads: you can change your ad consent choice at any time from Settings inside the app.
  • Advertising ID: Android lets you reset or delete it in your device settings.
  • Your jobs: delete any job in the app, or uninstall the app to remove all of them.

Changes

If what the app collects ever changes, this page and the Play Store data safety declaration are updated together, before the change ships.
